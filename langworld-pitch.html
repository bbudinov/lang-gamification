LangWorld

Learn languages by living in them.

A game world where kids practice speaking, listening, reading and writing in a new language — through immersive scenarios, not textbooks.

02 / 14

The Problem

Language apps teach words.
Not language.

  • Kids memorize "apple = manzana" but can't say "Can I have an apple?"
  • Most apps are multiple choice quizzes — no speaking, no listening, no real practice
  • The #1 complaint worldwide: "I understand but I'm afraid to speak"
  • Age 7-14 is almost completely ignored — apps are either for toddlers or adults

There's no product that makes kids actually PRACTICE using a language in real, meaningful scenarios.

03 / 14

The Solution

9 exercise types.
Every language skill. Through play.

Listening

  • Listen & Repeat — hear a phrase, say it back
  • Word Quiz — hear a word, pick the translation

Speaking & Pronunciation

  • Say It! — syllable-by-syllable feedback
  • NPC Talk — AI conversation with 3D avatar

Reading

  • Fill the Scene — complete sentences
  • True or False — judge translations

Writing / Spelling

  • Word Scramble — arrange letters
  • Memory Match — find word pairs
  • Memory Mix — cross-topic challenge

Competition

What exists vs what we built.

vs Duolingo

Multiple choice quiz app. No real speaking practice. LangWorld has syllable-level pronunciation + free-form AI conversation.

vs Lingokids / Khan Academy Kids

Ages 3-6 (preschool). LangWorld targets 7-14 — an underserved market worth billions.

vs ChatGPT / AI Tutors

Chat interfaces, not games. Kids don't want to "chat with AI" — they want to PLAY.

vs Praktika.ai

Adult-focused, subscription-heavy. LangWorld is kid-first, game-first, scenario-based. Both have 3D avatars — we add the game world.
