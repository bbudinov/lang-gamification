The Problem

Language apps teach words.
Not language.

  • Kids memorize "apple = ябълка" but can't say "Can I have an apple?"
  • Most apps are multiple choice quizzes — no speaking, no listening, no real practice
  • The #1 complaint worldwide: "I understand but I'm afraid to speak"
  • Age 7–14 is almost completely ignored — apps are either for toddlers or adults
  • Existing tools don't make kids WANT to practice — they feel like homework

There's no product that makes kids actually PRACTICE using a language in real, meaningful scenarios.

03 / 15

The Solution

9 exercise types.
Every language skill. Through play.

Listening

  • Listen & Repeat — hear, say it back, get scored
  • Word Quiz — hear a word, pick the translation

Speaking & Pronunciation

  • Say It! — syllable-by-syllable pronunciation feedback
  • NPC Talk — real AI conversation with 3D avatar

Reading

  • Fill the Scene — read and pick the missing word
  • True or False — judge word-translation pairs

Writing / Spelling

  • Word Scramble — arrange letters to spell words
  • Memory Match — find matching word pairs
  • Memory Mix — cross-topic card challenge
04 / 15

How It Works

Open. Play. Learn.

1. Choose from 16 themed 3D worlds
2. Each world has its own custom 3D map
3. Tap a location → pick a game → play
4. Earn XP → pass World Exams → unlock worlds
5. 3 levels: Discover → Use → Survive
6. Professor Globe guides with lip-synced speech

No accounts required. No setup. Just play.
Optional: kid-friendly PIN login for cloud sync.

05 / 15

The AI Teacher

Professor Globe

A full 3D character — not a chatbot.

  • Real-time lip sync matched to speech output
  • Eye blinking and natural idle animations
  • Head tracking (follows the camera)
  • AI-powered conversations (Claude API) — not scripted
  • Never punishes mistakes — always encourages
  • Encyclopedia mode: ask about ANYTHING

Built with React Three Fiber + Rhubarb Lip Sync. 168 pre-generated lip sync data files for word-level accuracy.

Why LangWorld Wins

Key Differentiators

vs Duolingo

Duolingo = multiple choice quiz app. No real speaking. No contextual learning. LangWorld has syllable-level pronunciation + free-form AI conversations in 3D worlds.

vs Lingokids

Lingokids targets ages 3–6. LangWorld targets 7–14 — an underserved market with real AI conversations, not pre-scripted animations.

vs ChatGPT / AI Tutors

Chat interfaces, not games. Kids don't want to "chat with AI" — they want to PLAY. No pronunciation feedback, no game mechanics, no 3D worlds.

vs Praktika.ai

Adult-focused, subscription-heavy. LangWorld is kid-first, game-first, with 16 worlds + 9 exercise types — not just conversation practice.

LangWorld = 16 3D worlds + 9 exercises + 154 AI NPCs + pronunciation feedback + 2,290+ words. In one product, for an underserved age group.
