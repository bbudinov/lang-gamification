LangWorld

Learn languages by playing in them.

A game world where kids practice speaking, listening, reading and writing in a new language — through real exercises, not textbooks.

The Problem

Language apps today teach words. Not language.

Kids memorize "apple = ябълка" but can't say "Can I have an apple?"


  • Most apps are multiple choice quizzes — no speaking, no real practice
  • The #1 global complaint: "I understand, but I'm afraid to speak"
  • Age 6–10 is almost empty — apps are for toddlers or adults
  • No product makes kids actually practice using a language

8 Exercise Types. Every Skill.

👂 Listening

Listen & Repeat — hear a phrase, say it back
Word Quiz — hear a word, pick the translation


🗣️ Speaking

Say It! — pronounce words, get feedback
NPC Talk — AI conversation with a character

📖 Reading

Fill the Scene — read, pick the missing word
True or False — judge word-translation pairs


✍️ Spelling

Word Scramble — arrange letters correctly
Memory Match — find matching word pairs

How It Works

  • Open the app → 3D island map with 12 topic islands
  • Tap an island — Animals, Food, Colors, Family...
  • Pick a game type → play 5–8 rounds
  • Earn points → unlock new islands
  • 3 levels per island: Discover → Use → Survive
  • Professor Globe guides you with voice + animations

No accounts. No setup. Just play.

🔮

Professor Globe — AI Guide

  • Animated energy sphere character with emotions
  • Speaks every word and sentence in 3 languages
  • Reacts to answers — happy, thinking, surprised
  • Eyes follow your finger on screen
  • In NPC Talk: has real AI conversations with the child
  • Never punishes mistakes — always encourages

Who Is It For

🎯 Primary: Kids 6–10

Learning English, Bulgarian, or Spanish through a game they actually want to play.

Safe. No ads. No social. No in-app purchases.

🌍 Also works for

Anyone who wants to practice speaking a language. Beginners who know words but can't use them. People with speaking anxiety — a safe space to practice.


The exercises are universal. The game wrapper makes it fun for kids.
